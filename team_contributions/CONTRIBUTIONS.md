
# Project Contributions

This section summarizes the responsibilities and contributions of each team member during the development of the SEISMOSCOPE project.

## Member 1 – Başak Karaman
**Data Processing and Statistical Analysis**

- Dataset exploration and understanding.
- Data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Statistical analysis of earthquake records.
- Preparation and organization of data for visualization.

## Member 2 – Meleksu Büyük
**Multidimensional and 3D Analysis**

- Development of Treemap visualization.
- Implementation of Parallel Coordinates visualization.
- Development of 3D Scatter Plot visualization.
- Analysis of multidimensional relationships between earthquake parameters.
- Comparison of filtered and unfiltered earthquake data.
- Examination of earthquake magnitude, depth, and geographical relationships.

## Member 3 – Rohat Sarıgül
**Geographical and Temporal Analysis**

- Development of geographical visualizations.
- Analysis of the spatial distribution of earthquakes.
- Examination of temporal earthquake patterns.
- Visualization of geographical and time-based trends.

## Team Contribution

All team members contributed to the development of SEISMOSCOPE by working on their assigned modules and integrating their work into the main application.

The final dashboard combines these individual contributions into an interactive earthquake data analysis platform.
